Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Founder's Seat collects and how it is used, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and optionally your college or company. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Usage: the days you sign in, and for each game the scenario seed, month reached, and final score.
  • Payments: an order reference, the amount and the payment status returned by PhonePe. We do not receive or store card numbers, UPI IDs or bank details.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, the free trial and paid access.
  • To count players and understand how the game is used, so we can improve it.
  • If your programme gave you access, to show your faculty your progress and results in their cohort.

We do not sell your data and do not send marketing emails.

Where it is stored

Account and game data are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Payments are handled by PhonePe. Your game in progress is also saved in your own browser.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to kokosheesh@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
