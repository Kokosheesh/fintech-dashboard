How this page works

Figures come from primary releases — NPCI, RBI, AMFI, NSE, MFIN — and from reporting that cites them. ✓ marks a figure checked against its source; ≈ marks the latest reported estimate or a value interpolated between releases. Each tab shows when its data was last refreshed, and the dot turns amber, then red, when a release is overdue.
